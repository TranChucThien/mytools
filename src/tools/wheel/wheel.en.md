## How to spin the wheel

1. Type or paste your options into **Entries (one per line)**: names, prizes, restaurants, anything.
2. Check **Remove the winner after each spin** if each entry should only win once.
3. Press **Spin** and watch the wheel slow down.
4. Read the winner shown under the wheel.

## How the wheel picks a winner

Many people assume a spinning wheel is decided by how hard you spin it. Here the winner is chosen **first**, using crypto.getRandomValues, the secure random number generator built into your browser. Only then does the wheel animate and stop on that slice. The animation is for fun and has no effect on fairness.

Each entry gets an equal slice:

| Entries on the wheel | Chance for each entry |
| --- | --- |
| 4 | 1/4 |
| 5 | 1/5 |
| 10 | 1/10 |

## Popular uses

- **Livestream giveaways**: spin for a winner live in front of your audience.
- **Classroom**: pick a student to answer, choose a quiz question or presentation topic.
- **What should we eat?**: list a few places or dishes and let the wheel decide.
- **Team activities**: choose who presents first, run a prize draw at the end of a meeting or party.

## Tips for a fair-looking draw

- Show the full list before spinning so people can check their names.
- Share or record your screen while you press **Spin**.
- For several prizes, turn on **Remove the winner after each spin** and go from the smallest prize to the biggest.
