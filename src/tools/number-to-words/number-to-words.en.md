## How to use the number to words converter

1. Type a whole number into **Number**. Commas are fine: 1250000 and 1,250,000 give the same result.
2. Turn on **Capitalize first letter** if the words start a sentence or an amount line.
3. Read the result under **In words**.

## How numbers are spelled out

| Rule | Example |
| --- | --- |
| Hyphen between tens and ones from 21 to 99 | 21: twenty-one, 99: ninety-nine |
| No "and" after hundred (US style) | 105: one hundred five |
| Groups of three digits get a scale word | 1,250,000: one million two hundred fifty thousand |
| Empty groups are skipped | 2,000,024: two million twenty-four |

The scales used are **thousand**, **million**, **billion** and **trillion**, following the short scale used in the US and in modern British English, where a billion is a thousand million.

## When to spell out numbers

- **Writing a check:** the written amount is the legal one if it differs from the figures, so it has to be exact. 1,250 becomes "one thousand two hundred fifty".
- **Contracts and invoices:** many templates repeat key amounts in words to prevent tampering, for example "One million two hundred fifty thousand (1,250,000)".
- **Learning English numbers:** check how a large number is read aloud and where the hyphens go. 105 is "one hundred five" and 21 is "twenty-one".

> Tip: for check amounts with cents, write the dollars in words from this tool and add the cents as a fraction, such as "and 25/100".
