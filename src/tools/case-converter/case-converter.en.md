## How to change text case

1. Type or paste your text into the input box.
2. Press one of the buttons: **UPPERCASE**, **lowercase**, **Sentence case**, **Title Case** or **tOGGLE cASE**.
3. The result appears instantly below. Press **Copy** to paste it into your document, email or spreadsheet.

## What each option does

Starting from: "the quick brown fox. it jumps over Paris!"

| Option | Result |
| --- | --- |
| **UPPERCASE** | THE QUICK BROWN FOX. IT JUMPS OVER PARIS! |
| **lowercase** | the quick brown fox. it jumps over paris! |
| **Sentence case** | The quick brown fox. It jumps over paris! |
| **Title Case** | The Quick Brown Fox. It Jumps Over Paris! |
| **tOGGLE cASE** | THE QUICK BROWN FOX. IT JUMPS OVER pARIS! |

Notice that **Sentence case** turns "Paris" into "paris": the tool follows fixed rules and cannot tell which words are proper nouns.

## When a case converter saves time

- **Caps Lock accidents**: fix a whole paragraph without retyping it.
- **Names and lists**: turn "JOHN SMITH" or "john smith" into "John Smith" for mailing lists and sign-in sheets.
- **Headings and titles**: switch a heading to UPPERCASE or Title Case in one click.
- **Cleaning data**: lowercase email addresses or product codes before pasting them into a spreadsheet.

## Tips

- With messy mixed-case text, press **lowercase** first for a clean base, then pick the case you want.
- After **Sentence case**, skim the result and recapitalize names, places and acronyms like "USA".
- All-caps paragraphs are hard to read, so keep UPPERCASE for short headings and labels.
