## How to make random teams

1. Paste names into **Members (one per line)**.
2. Choose a mode: **By number of teams** or **By team size**.
3. Enter the **Number of teams** or **People per team**, depending on the mode.
4. Press **Make teams** and read out each team.

## How teams are made

First the whole list is shuffled with crypto.getRandomValues, the secure random number generator in your browser. Then members are dealt round-robin into the teams, so team sizes never differ by more than one person.

| Group | Setting | Result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 10 people | 3 teams | 4, 3, 3 |
| 12 people | 3 teams | 4, 4, 4 |
| 14 people | 4 teams | 4, 4, 3, 3 |
| 10 people | 4 per team | 3 teams: 4, 3, 3 |

## Popular uses

- **Pickup sports**: split players for football, badminton or basketball without anyone picking sides.
- **Classroom**: create groups for projects and discussions that mix students beyond their usual friends.
- **Team building**: mix people from different departments at company events and workshops.
- **Games and outings**: form teams for quizzes, party games or camping trips.

## Tips for fair and fun teams

- Agree on the mode and number of teams before pressing **Make teams**.
- Share your screen so everyone sees the result at the same time.
- To balance skill, split the strongest players first in one run, then the rest in a second run.
