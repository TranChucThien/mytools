## How to convert kg to lbs

Type a value in the **kg** box and the weight in pounds appears instantly in the **lbs** box. You can also type into the lbs box to convert the other way, or press **Swap** to open the LBS to KG page.

## KG to LBS formula

> **lbs = kg ÷ 0.45359237** (≈ kg × 2.20462)

The factor comes from the international definition 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg, so 1 kg = 1 ÷ 0.45359237 ≈ 2.20462 lbs.

## Examples

- Body weight **70 kg** = 70 × 2.20462 ≈ **154.32 lbs**
- Checked bag **23 kg** = **50.71 lbs** (the common 50 lb airline limit)
- Weight plate **20 kg** ≈ **44.09 lbs**
- Newborn baby **3.4 kg** ≈ **7.50 lbs**
