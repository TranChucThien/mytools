## How to use the word counter

Type or paste your text into the box. The stats update as you type:

- **Words**: groups of characters separated by spaces.
- **Characters**: every character, including spaces.
- **Characters (no spaces)**: letters, numbers and punctuation only.
- **Sentences**: based on periods, question marks and exclamation marks.
- **Paragraphs**: blocks of text separated by line breaks.
- **Reading time**: estimated at 200 words per minute.

## How counting works

| Text | Words | Characters | Characters (no spaces) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I love coffee | 3 | 13 | 11 |
| A well-known fact. | 3 | 18 | 16 |

Hyphenated words count as one word. Reading time is simply words ÷ 200, so 400 words is about 2 minutes and 850 words is about 4.25 minutes.

## When a word count matters

- **Essays and applications:** college essays, cover letters and scholarship forms often have strict word limits.
- **SEO:** keep meta descriptions around 160 characters and page titles short so they are not truncated in search results.
- **Social media:** X (Twitter), LinkedIn and other platforms limit post length or only show the first lines, so lead with the key point.
- **Speeches and videos:** a 1,500-word script takes roughly 7.5 minutes at 200 words per minute, which helps you plan timing.
