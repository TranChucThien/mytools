## How to use the days between dates calculator

1. Choose the **Start date**, for example today or the first day of a project.
2. Choose the **End date**, such as a deadline, a holiday or an exam.
3. Tick **Include end date** if the last day should count as a full day. Leave it off for a countdown.
4. Read the **Days**, **Weeks**, **Months and days** and **Weekdays (Mon to Fri)**.

## What each result means

| Result | Meaning | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days | Total number of days between the dates | 84 days |
| Weeks | Whole weeks plus leftover days | 12 weeks 0 days |
| Months and days | Whole calendar months from the start date, plus leftover days | 2 months 23 days |
| Weekdays (Mon to Fri) | Days that are not Saturday or Sunday | Holidays not removed |

If the **End date** is earlier than the **Start date**, the tool shows the same counts as with the dates swapped.

## Examples

- **Countdown to the end of the year:** October 8, 2026 to December 31, 2026, without the end date, is **84 days**, or 12 weeks, or 2 months and 23 days, with 60 weekdays. With **Include end date** ticked it is 85 days.
- **New Year to Lunar New Year:** January 1, 2026 to February 17, 2026 (the first day of the Lunar New Year in 2026) is **47 days**, or 6 weeks and 5 days, or 1 month and 16 days, with 33 weekdays.
- **Project deadline:** check **Weekdays (Mon to Fri)** to see how many working days you have, then subtract any public holidays in that period.

> Tip: the weekday count does not know your local holidays or company days off. January 1, 2026 is a Thursday, so it is counted as a weekday even though it is a holiday in many countries.
