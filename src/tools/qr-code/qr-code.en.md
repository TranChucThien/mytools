## How to create a QR code online

1. Paste a **link** or type any **text** – your QR code appears instantly.
2. Adjust the **size**, **code color**, **background** and **error correction** if you like.
3. Press **Download PNG** or **Download SVG** to save it.
4. Test it with your phone camera before printing or sharing.

## What can you put in a QR code?

- **Website, social profile or online store links** – one scan opens the page.
- **Google Forms or Drive links** – collect responses or share files.
- **Phone numbers** – enter `tel:+15551234567` so scanning starts a call.
- **Email addresses** – enter `mailto:you@example.com`.
- **Any text** – product codes, Wi-Fi hints, short messages.

## Tips for QR codes that scan reliably

- Shorter content makes a less dense, easier-to-scan code – shorten long URLs.
- When printing, make the code at least **2 × 2 cm (0.8 in)** with a white margin around it.
- Use **error correction Q or H** for codes placed outdoors or on surfaces that may get scratched.
