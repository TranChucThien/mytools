## How to use the gross to net salary calculator

1. Pick a mode:
   - **Gross to Net**: you know the contract salary and want to see the take-home pay.
   - **Net to Gross**: you know the take-home pay you want and need the matching gross salary.
2. Enter the **Gross salary** (or **Net salary** in Net to Gross mode), for example 30,000,000.
3. Enter the number of **Dependents** registered for family deduction.
4. Choose the **Region** where you work: I, II, III or IV.
5. If your employer pays insurance on a lower amount than your actual salary, enter the **Insurance salary**. Leave it blank to use the gross salary.
6. Read the **Net salary** (or **Gross salary**), **BHXH (8%)**, **Health insurance (1.5%)**, **Unemployment insurance (1%)**, **Taxable income**, **Personal income tax** and the tax breakdown per bracket.

## How net salary is calculated in Vietnam

In Vietnam, salaries are usually quoted gross. The employer deducts the employee share of compulsory insurance and withholds personal income tax (PIT) before paying the net amount.

| Step | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| Insurance | `Social insurance (BHXH) = 8% × min(Insurance salary, 50,600,000)` |
| | `Health insurance = 1.5% × min(Insurance salary, 50,600,000)` |
| | `Unemployment insurance = 1% × min(Insurance salary, 20 × regional minimum wage)` |
| Taxable income | `Gross − Insurance − 15,500,000 − 6,200,000 × Dependents` (not below 0) |
| PIT | 5-bracket progressive schedule applied to taxable income |
| Net salary | `Gross − Insurance − PIT` |

In **Net to Gross** mode the calculator searches numerically for the gross salary that produces your target net after insurance and tax.

**PIT schedule on salary (monthly taxable income):**

| Bracket | Monthly taxable income | Rate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Up to 10,000,000 VND | 5% |
| 2 | Over 10,000,000 to 30,000,000 VND | 10% |
| 3 | Over 30,000,000 to 60,000,000 VND | 20% |
| 4 | Over 60,000,000 to 100,000,000 VND | 30% |
| 5 | Over 100,000,000 VND | 35% |

**Unemployment insurance cap by region (from 1 January 2026):**

| Region | Regional minimum wage | Unemployment insurance cap |
| --- | --- | --- |
| I | 5,310,000 VND | 106,200,000 VND |
| II | 4,730,000 VND | 94,600,000 VND |
| III | 4,140,000 VND | 82,800,000 VND |
| IV | 3,700,000 VND | 74,000,000 VND |

## Worked examples

**Example 1: gross 30,000,000 VND, no dependents, Region I**

- Insurance: 2,400,000 + 450,000 + 300,000 = 3,150,000 VND.
- Taxable income: 30,000,000 − 3,150,000 − 15,500,000 = 11,350,000 VND.
- PIT: 10,000,000 × 5% + 1,350,000 × 10% = 500,000 + 135,000 = 635,000 VND.
- Net salary: 30,000,000 − 3,150,000 − 635,000 = **26,215,000 VND**.

**Example 2: gross 100,000,000 VND, 1 dependent, Region I**

- Social and health insurance on the 50,600,000 cap: 4,048,000 + 759,000 = 4,807,000 VND.
- Unemployment insurance on 100,000,000 (below the 106,200,000 cap): 1,000,000 VND.
- Total insurance: 5,807,000 VND.
- Taxable income: 100,000,000 − 5,807,000 − 15,500,000 − 6,200,000 = 72,493,000 VND.

| Bracket | Income in bracket | Tax |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (5%) | 10,000,000 VND | 500,000 VND |
| 2 (10%) | 20,000,000 VND | 2,000,000 VND |
| 3 (20%) | 30,000,000 VND | 6,000,000 VND |
| 4 (30%) | 12,493,000 VND | 3,747,900 VND |
| Total | 72,493,000 VND | 12,247,900 VND |

- Net salary: 100,000,000 − 5,807,000 − 12,247,900 = **81,945,100 VND**.

## Notes for expats and employers

Results are estimates for tax residents with a labour contract of 3 months or more. The calculator does not cover tax-exempt allowances (such as meal allowances within the legal limit), the 20% flat rate for non-residents or the 10% withholding on contracts shorter than 3 months.

The social and health insurance cap used here is 50,600,000 VND, based on the base salary of 2,530,000 VND from 1 July 2026. From 1 January to 30 June 2026 the cap was 46,800,000 VND, so payslips for the first half of 2026 may differ slightly for high earners.

The rules come from the Personal Income Tax Law No. 109/2025/QH15, Resolution 110/2025/UBTVQH15 on family deductions, Decree 161/2026/ND-CP on the base salary and Decree 293/2025/ND-CP on regional minimum wages.

> Note: insurance and tax rules can change. This page reflects the rules as of October 2026. Confirm the figures with HR or a tax adviser before you sign a contract or run payroll.
