## How to convert lbs to kg

Type a value in the **lbs** box and the weight in kilograms appears instantly in the **kg** box. You can also type into the kg box to convert the other way, or press **Swap** to open the KG to LBS page.

## LBS to KG formula

> **kg = lbs × 0.45359237** (or **kg = lbs ÷ 2.20462**)

0.45359237 is the exact value of one international pound, fixed by agreement in 1959.

## Examples

- Bag of flour **2 lbs** ≈ **0.91 kg**
- Tub of protein powder **5 lbs** ≈ **2.27 kg**
- Airline bag limit **50 lbs** ≈ **22.68 kg**
- Body weight **180 lbs** ≈ **81.65 kg**
