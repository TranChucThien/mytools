## How to use the BMI calculator

1. Enter your **Weight (kg)**, for example 85 or 72.5.
2. Enter your **Height (cm)**, for example 180.
3. Choose a **Classification**: **WHO international** (the default) or **Asian**.
4. You instantly see your BMI, your weight category and the healthy weight range for your height.

## BMI formula and categories

Formula: `BMI = weight (kg) ÷ (height (m) × height (m))`

| Category | WHO international | Asian |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Underweight | Below 18.5 | Below 18.5 |
| Normal weight | 18.5 to 24.9 | 18.5 to 22.9 |
| Overweight | 25 to 29.9 | 23 to 24.9 |
| Obese | 30 or more | 25 or more |

The Asian classification follows the WHO Western Pacific Region and IDI recommendations from 2000. If you are of East or Southeast Asian descent, it gives a more relevant picture of risk.

## Examples

- **85 kg, 180 cm:** BMI = 85 ÷ 1.80² ≈ 26.2, overweight under WHO. The normal range for 180 cm is about 59.9 to 80.7 kg.
- **72 kg, 170 cm:** BMI ≈ 24.9. That is normal under WHO but **overweight** under the Asian classification, which is why choosing the right standard matters.
- **60 kg, 165 cm:** BMI ≈ 22.0, normal under both classifications.

> Note: BMI is a quick screening measure, not a medical diagnosis. It does not separate muscle from fat and is not meant for children or pregnant women. If you are concerned about your weight, talk to a healthcare professional.
