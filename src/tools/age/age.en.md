## How to use the age calculator

1. Pick or type your birthday in **Date of birth**.
2. **Age at date** defaults to today. Change it if you need your age on another day, like a visa application, a race entry or a school cutoff date.
3. The results update instantly: your exact age in **years, months and days**, total days lived, days until your next birthday and the **weekday you were born**.

## How the age is calculated

The calculator counts full years first, then full months, then the remaining days, the same way you would count on a calendar:

| Step | What is counted |
| --- | --- |
| Years | Birthdays that have already passed up to the chosen date |
| Months | Full months since the last birthday |
| Days | Days left over after the last full month |
| Total days | Every calendar day between the two dates |

For leap day babies born on February 29, the birthday is counted on **February 28** in common years. So the next birthday after October 8, 2026 falls on February 28, 2027.

## Examples

- **Born August 15, 1995, age on October 8, 2026:** 31 years, 1 month, 23 days. That is 11,377 days, born on a Tuesday, with 311 days to go until the next birthday.
- **Born February 29, 2000, age on October 8, 2026:** born on a Tuesday, 9,718 days lived. The next birthday is February 28, 2027, 143 days away.
- **School cutoff check:** a child born May 20, 2010 is 16 years, 4 months and 18 days old on October 8, 2026.

> Tip: for forms that say "must be at least 18" or "under 35 on the closing date", set **Age at date** to the deadline itself rather than today, so you know for sure whether you qualify.
