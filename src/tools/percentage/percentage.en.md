faq:
  - q: How do I calculate a percentage of a number?
    a: Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example, 15% of 200 is 200 × 15 ÷ 100 = 30.
  - q: How do I find what percent one number is of another?
    a: Divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100. For example, 45 out of 60 is 45 ÷ 60 × 100 = 75%.
  - q: How do I calculate percentage increase or decrease?
    a: Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. A positive result is an increase, a negative one a decrease. Going from $50 to $65 is a (65 − 50) ÷ 50 × 100 = 30% increase.
  - q: If something goes up 20% and then down 20%, is it back where it started?
    a: No. 100 up 20% is 120, and 20% off 120 is 96. The decrease is taken from the larger number, so you end up below where you started.

  - q: Can I enter decimals and thousands separators?
    a: Yes. On the English page use a dot for decimals (12.5) and optionally commas for thousands (1,250,000).

## How to use the percentage calculator

Pick the question you want to answer and fill in the blanks. The answer appears as you type, together with the formula so you can double-check it:

1. **X% of Y**: e.g. how much is a 30% tip on a $85 bill?
2. **X is what % of Y**: e.g. what percentage is 18 correct answers out of 25?
3. **Percentage change**: e.g. rent went from $1,400 to $1,512; what's the percentage increase?

Hit **Copy** to paste the result into a spreadsheet, chat or report.

## Percentage formulas

| Problem | Formula |
| --- | --- |
| X% of Y | `Y × X ÷ 100` |
| X is what % of Y | `X ÷ Y × 100` |
| Percentage change from A to B | `(B − A) ÷ \|A\| × 100` |

## Examples

- **Shopping:** a $120 jacket with 25% off → you save 120 × 25 ÷ 100 = $30 and pay $90.
- **Grades:** 42 out of 50 on a test → 42 ÷ 50 × 100 = 84%.
- **Business:** revenue fell from $80,000 to $68,000 → (68,000 − 80,000) ÷ 80,000 × 100 = −15%, a 15% decrease.
- **Tipping:** 18% of a $64 restaurant bill → $11.52.

> Quick mental math: 10% of a number is just the number with the decimal point moved one place left (10% of 450 is 45). Double it for 20%, halve it for 5%.
